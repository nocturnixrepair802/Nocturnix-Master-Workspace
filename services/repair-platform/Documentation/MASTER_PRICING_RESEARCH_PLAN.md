# Master Pricing Research Plan

## Purpose and Scope

This document defines the planning framework for collecting regional competitor pricing and evaluating Nocturnix pricing inputs. It is a research plan only. It does not authorize or begin Phase 1B implementation, modify pricing workbooks, or establish final customer prices.

## Research Regions

Pricing research will cover:

- Vermont
- New Hampshire
- Maine
- Upstate New York
- Western Massachusetts

Research should include competitors from a representative mix of cities and communities in each region where reliable public pricing is available.

## Priority Service Categories

Research should prioritize these service categories:

1. iPhone repairs
2. Samsung repairs
3. Google Pixel repairs
4. Tablet repairs
5. Laptop repairs
6. Desktop repairs
7. Gaming console repairs

Within each category, comparable device models and repair services should be used whenever possible. Differences in model, part quality, warranty, turnaround time, and service scope must be recorded rather than treated as equivalent offers.

## Competitor Pricing Fields

Each competitor pricing observation should include the following fields:

| Field | Collection guidance |
| --- | --- |
| Competitor | Business name offering the service. |
| City/State | Location associated with the advertised price. |
| Device | Brand and model, including relevant variant when available. |
| Service | Specific repair or diagnostic service advertised. |
| Advertised Price | Publicly advertised price, including qualifiers such as starting price, promotion, or diagnostic fee. |
| Warranty | Advertised warranty term and coverage, if stated. |
| Part Quality (if known) | OEM, refurbished, aftermarket, premium aftermarket, or other disclosed quality level. |
| Turnaround Time | Advertised or quoted completion time. |
| Date Collected | Date the observation was captured. |
| Source URL | Direct URL supporting the observation. |
| Notes | Taxes, fees, promotions, exclusions, uncertainty, bundled services, or other comparison context. |

## Internal Pricing Fields

Internal pricing analysis should use the following fields:

| Field | Planning purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Supplier Cost | Direct cost of the required part or component. |
| Shipping | Allocated inbound shipping or freight cost. |
| Consumables | Adhesives, cleaners, fasteners, thermal materials, and other job-specific supplies. |
| Labor Time | Estimated hands-on and allocated service time. |
| Labor Rate | Internal hourly labor rate used for the calculation. |
| Overhead | Allocated operating cost associated with completing the service. |
| Target Margin | Desired margin used for pricing analysis. |
| Recommended Nocturnix Price | Proposed price produced after reviewing internal cost, risk, value, and market context. |

Calculation assumptions and units should be documented consistently so that recommendations can be reviewed and reproduced.

## Research Standards

- Use publicly available pricing or documented direct quotes from identifiable competitors.
- Record one observation per competitor, location, device, and service combination.
- Preserve the collection date and direct source URL for auditability.
- Distinguish fixed prices from estimates, ranges, promotions, and "starting at" prices.
- Do not infer warranty, part quality, fees, or turnaround time when a source does not disclose them; record the value as unknown.
- Capture enough notes to explain material differences that affect price comparability.
- Revalidate time-sensitive observations before using them in a pricing review.

## Pricing Governance

Competitor pricing is market research only and does not automatically determine customer pricing. Recommended Nocturnix prices must also consider verified internal costs, labor requirements, overhead, target margin, warranty exposure, repair complexity, operational risk, service value, and applicable business rules. Final customer pricing requires separate review and approval.

## Planned Research Deliverables

The research phase should produce:

- A traceable set of competitor observations covering the defined regions and priority service categories.
- A comparable internal-cost record for each service evaluated.
- A documented pricing recommendation with assumptions, exceptions, and confidence notes.
- A coverage review identifying missing regions, categories, devices, or unreliable comparisons.

## Phase Boundary

Phase 1B implementation is explicitly out of scope for this planning task and must not begin as part of this document. Any workbook population, application changes, automated collection, pricing-engine work, or operational rollout requires separate authorization.
