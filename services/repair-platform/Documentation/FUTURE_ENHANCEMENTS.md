# Future Enhancements

Last updated: 2026-07-22

These items are intentionally outside the active stabilization milestone.

## Operations

- Inventory purchasing, reorder alerts, valuation, and supplier management.
- Estimates, invoices, payments, refunds, and warranty tracking.
- Technician scheduling, assignments, time tracking, and workload reporting.
- Customer communications and repair-status notifications.

## Reporting and analytics

- Revenue, margin, turnaround-time, and repair-volume dashboards.
- Inventory forecasting and low-stock prediction.
- Device/common-failure trend analysis.
- Exportable operational and accounting reports.

## Platform

- Replaceable SQLite, PostgreSQL, or service-backed persistence.
- Multi-user access, authentication, roles, and audit logs.
- Cloud synchronization and offline conflict handling.
- Automated installer, updates, backups, and recovery tooling.
- REST API and integration with POS/accounting systems.

## Customer and technician experiences

- Customer portal, online booking, estimates, and repair tracking.
- Mobile technician companion application.
- Barcode/QR intake, inventory, and ticket labels.
- Photo and document attachments.

## Knowledge and automation

- Searchable repair guides and technical-document library.
- AI-assisted diagnostics with human confirmation and traceable sources.
- Suggested parts, labor, pricing, and repair procedures.
- Automated compatibility and quality-control checklists.
