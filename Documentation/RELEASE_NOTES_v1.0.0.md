# Nocturnix Repair Platform v1.0.0

Release Date: 2026-07-23

## Release Scope

Version 1.0.0 establishes the governed Catalog Foundation for the Nocturnix Repair Platform.

This release provides review-oriented generation and independent validation pipelines for:

- Master Services
- Master Pricing
- Master Parts
- Master Devices
- Master Compatibility

## Validated Catalog Totals

- Master Services: 314 records
- Master Pricing: 314 records
- Master Parts: 48 records
- Master Devices: 46 records
- Master Compatibility: 352 proposed relationships
- Compatibility unresolved review candidates: 10

## Identifier Ranges

- Services: SVC000076 through SVC000389
- Pricing: PRC000001 through PRC000314
- Parts: PRT000001 through PRT000048
- Devices: DEV000843 through DEV000888
- Compatibility: CMP000083 through CMP000434

## Governance and Safety

- Canonical workbooks remain read-only.
- Protected inputs are verified with SHA-256 hashes.
- Generated workbooks use transactional output handling.
- Independent validators verify workbook structure, identifiers, provenance, relationships, controlled values, and defined-name validations.
- Legacy prices and costs remain provisional observations.
- No final pricing approval was performed.
- No inventory update was performed.
- No canonical import was performed.

## Known Review Work

- All 314 services remain pending labor mapping.
- Pricing records require cost, labor, margin, market, and approval policy decisions.
- Parts require sourcing, quality, OEM, cost, and device-compatibility review.
- Devices require manufacturer alias and model/variant review.
- Compatibility relationships are currently family-level proposals.
- Ten compatibility candidates remain unresolved.

## Release Boundary

Version 1.0.0 completes the Catalog Foundation only.

It does not include:

- canonical import execution;
- production pricing;
- inventory operations;
- repair-ticket workflows;
- customer portal functionality;
- Square synchronization;
- website integration;
- production API services.
